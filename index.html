Such Life

Such Life is a 2D top-down sandbox game made in Unity with C#. The player can explore an overworld, fighting through randomly generated dungeons and crafting items and equipment to progress.

• Developed multiple integrated systems such as a graph-based dungeon generator and chunk manager/loader.
• lead the project in 2025, where I managed and instructed new members to implement features like combat additions and a consumable item system. Still participating at present.

Tech stack: Unity, C#

Dream Diver

DreamDiver is a first-person, grid-based action game developed in Unity that has the player navigating dungeons, fighting enemies with the cursor, and collecting equipment and currency to become stronger. Developed entirely by myself!

• Developed a system of integrated gameplay systems and mechanics to facilitate player exploration through dungeons, combat with enemies, and power progression
• Created visual assets such as UI and 3D models to aid the gameplay and provide a sense of 'place' and setting within the game

Tech stack: Unity, C#, Blender, Adobe Illustrator

Skquid

Skquid is a social media web platform that allows users to connect via avenues of event and skill sharing. Users can create accounts and find and chat with other users searchable by skill or other filters. They can also create event groups with other users to meet up with.

• I implemented the messaging page functionality using WebSockets and MongoDB collections.
• Users can have live chats with each other on the site, and send and receive new messages to friends or users they find through searching.

Tech stack: CSS/HTML/JS, MongoDB, Express, NodeJS, React
